\begin{abstract}
We propose and validate \emph{Young's Entanglement-Driven Gravity (EDG)}, a covariant relation in which spacetime curvature is sourced by quantum entanglement rather than classical stress-energy. Using time-evolved Projected Entangled Pair States (PEPS) under a local Heisenberg Hamiltonian, we show that entanglement structure generates AdS-like negative curvature, holographic entropy scaling, and Hawking/Page-like information dynamics. We provide two complementary EDG formulations: a \textbf{tensorial form} suitable for theory and a \textbf{parameterized form} designed for data-driven constraints. In the weak field, EDG reproduces all classical tests (perihelion shift, Shapiro delay, light bending) while in the strong field it predicts controlled deviations quantifiable by an entanglement length scale $L_q$ and index $p$. A 13-stage validation suite (R1--R13$^{+}$) culminates in R13$^{+}$, a joint posterior in $(\epsilon,L_q)$ that incorporates EHT ring measurements fractionally anchored to the Schwarzschild radius and toy GW phasing rows. The results favor $\epsilon\simeq1$ (GR-consistent) with a finite upper limit on $L_q$ at $\sim 10^{13}$~m for M87*, depending on the fractional prior; see Tables~\ref{tab:r13p_marginals}, \ref{tab:r13p_bestfits} and Fig.~\ref{fig:r13p_heat}. We discuss implications for holography, the black-hole information problem, and cosmology, and release code and data for full reproducibility.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation and Big Picture}
General Relativity (GR) geometrizes gravity but leaves quantum mechanics outside the metric. Quantum theory, in turn, treats spacetime as a fixed background. Bringing them into a single framework remains the central problem of fundamental physics. A broad set of ideas---holography, entanglement wedges, ER=EPR, entropic gravity, and Jacobson's thermodynamic derivation of Einstein's equation---all point to \emph{entanglement as the organizing principle of geometry}.
Building on this arc, we ask: can we write a concrete, testable relation in which curvature responds directly to quantum entanglement, and then validate it from quantum-scale simulations all the way to astronomical observations?

\section{The EDG Framework}
\subsection{Tensorial (theory) form}
We posit
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:edg_tensor}
G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} \;=\; -\,\alpha\, E_{\mu\nu},
\end{equation}
where $E_{\mu\nu}$ is an \emph{entanglement tensor} constructed from reduced states and modular flows on spacelike slices, and $\alpha$ is a coupling fixed phenomenologically by quantum simulations and consistency with low-curvature tests.
In discrete PEPS experiments we approximate $E_{\mu\nu}$ by local combinations of mutual informations $I(i\!:\!j)$ and their transport; curvature is discretized via a Regge/Ollivier-Ricci surrogate. This closes the system and yields emergent AdS-like geometry from purely quantum inputs. Prior exposition and data from our earlier work provide an extended derivation and numerical evidence. \textit{See the detailed prior manuscript for methodology and results.} :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

\subsection{Parameterized (constraints) form}
For direct confrontation with data we employ a minimal map for strong-field observables:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:edg_param}
\mathcal{O}_{\rm EDG}(r_s)\;=\; \mathcal{O}_{\rm GR}(r_s)\,\Big[1 + \kappa \,\bigg(\frac{r_s}{L_q}\bigg)^{p}\Big],
\end{equation}
with $(\kappa,p)$ controlling the \emph{shape} of departures and $L_q$ the \emph{entanglement length}. In the weak-field post-Newtonian regime this reduces to PPN-like shifts (absorbed into $\epsilon$), ensuring consistency with Cassini $\gamma$, light bending, Shapiro delay, and classical perihelion tests.

\section{Emergent Geometry from PEPS under a Heisenberg Hamiltonian}
We evolve a PEPS on a $L_x\times L_y$ lattice with a nearest-neighbor Heisenberg Hamiltonian. At each step we compute $I(i\!:\!j)$, induce distances via $d\sim -\log I$, approximate curvature by an Ollivier-Ricci surrogate, and form a discrete $G(i)$ which we fit against $E(i)$ to extract $\alpha$. The 3x3 and 4x4 studies exhibit:
(i) negative curvature (AdS-like),
(ii) holographic entropy scaling (Ryu-Takayanagi),
(iii) a Page-like curve for mutual information across a horizon,
(iv) size-dependent disentanglement rates.

\section{Alignment with GR and Novel Predictions}
In the post-Newtonian regime ($r_s/L_q\!\ll\!1$) Eq.~\eqref{eq:edg_param} reduces to $\mathcal{O}_{\rm EDG}\!\approx\!\mathcal{O}_{\rm GR}$, explaining our clean agreement with R1--R12. Near horizons ($r_s/L_q\!\sim\!1$) EDG predicts controlled fractional shifts governed by $(\kappa,p)$; these can be probed by future EHT baselines and GW phasing/echo searches. EDG avoids GR pathologies by tying curvature to entanglement entropy: singularities are deferred where the quantum state cannot support arbitrarily large geometrical strain.

\section{Implications}
EDG provides a concrete map from entanglement to geometry, dovetailing with holography and Jacobson-like thermodynamics and suggesting routes to resolve information-loss tension. Cosmologically, a slow, global drift in entanglement could mimic dark components (speculative); observationally this reduces to bounds on $L_q$ via large-scale tests.
